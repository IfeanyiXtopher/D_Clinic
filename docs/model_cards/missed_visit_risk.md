# Model card — Missed-visit risk (`missed_visit_v1`)

## Model details

- **Task.** Estimate the probability that a scheduled follow-up visit in a hypertension/diabetes program will be missed (no BP recorded within −3 … +7 days of the scheduled date).
- **Type.** Logistic regression (scikit-learn) on 27 features, selected over a rule score and histogram gradient boosting because boosting did not beat it by more than 0.01 validation AUC. Gradient boosting is retained in the artifact for comparison.
- **Cold start.** Partial pooling (`ml/cold_start.py`): new patients receive the miss rate of similar patients (facility × diabetes × drug count × age band; back-off to facility × age band, facility, program), blended with personal history as visits accumulate (\(k = 3\) pseudo-observations). Output carries `basis ∈ {group, mixed, personal}` and `group_size`.
- **Explanations.** Top-3 risk-raising contributions of the logistic model, restricted to positive-coefficient, actionable features, rendered in plain language.
- **Bands.** `medium` ≥ 50th and `high` ≥ 80th percentile of training scores.
- **Code.** `D_Clinic_Backend/ml/{features,cold_start,train,predict}.py`. Artifact `ml/artifacts/missed_visit_v1.joblib`. Tracking: MLflow (`mlflow.db`, experiment `missed_visit_risk`).

## Intended use

- Order a health worker's daily/weekly follow-up list and flag visits that deserve an earlier reminder or a phone call.
- Decision support only. A score creates a task; it never removes anyone from care, changes treatment, or triggers an automated clinical action.

## Out-of-scope uses

- Any use that reduces service to a patient because of a high score.
- Individual-level "will this person come" claims to patients or third parties.
- Populations or programs whose data the model has not been evaluated on. This version is trained on **synthetic** data only.

## Training data

- Synthetic Simple-shaped cohort (`data/synth/generate.py`, seed 42): 6,000 patients, 5 facilities, 24 months, 36k labelled appointments, 29% missed. See `docs/definitions.md`.
- Features are computed strictly as of booking time (`tests/test_features.py` injects future events and asserts features are unchanged).
- Excluded from features: names, phone numbers, addresses, national ID, **region/ethnicity**. Region is used only in the fairness audit.

## Evaluation

Splits: patients hashed into pools (60/15/25) **and** time-cut (train < 2026-03-01; valid Mar–May 2026; test ≥ 2026-06-01). No patient in two splits.

Test set (n = 2,028, base rate 0.295):

| model | AUC | PR-AUC | Brier | precision@30% | lift |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rule score | 0.626 | 0.401 | – | 0.421 | 1.43 |
| **logistic (selected)** | **0.648** | **0.459** | 0.191 | **0.438** | **1.49** |
| gradient boosting | 0.649 | 0.462 | 0.191 | 0.433 | 1.47 |

Signal ceiling on this synthetic data (scoring with the generator's *hidden* per-patient propensity): AUC 0.681 alone, 0.704 with known lead-time/distance/streak effects. The selected model, using observable history only, is 0.05 below that ceiling. Real-program AUCs reported in the literature are 0.70–0.80 with richer data; those numbers are not comparable to a synthetic cohort.

Calibration: ECE ≈ 0.02 on test; cold-start rows (basis `group`) predicted 0.289 vs observed 0.280.

Full tables: `docs/eval_reports/missed_visit_model.md` (regenerated by `make train`).

## Fairness

Audited on test by sex, age band, facility and region: base rate, mean prediction, AUC, selection rate at 30% capacity, false-negative rate at capacity, ECE. Findings and discussion: `notebooks/03_fairness_and_calibration.ipynb`.

- Region is not a feature. The raw regional gap in miss rate is mediated by distance band (`addresses.zone`), which *is* a feature because travel burden is an actionable, non-protected reason to offer support.
- No group is under-selected relative to its miss rate beyond sampling noise on this cohort. Women are selected at their base rate (0.30); men slightly above theirs (0.35 vs 0.29), so their false-negative rate at capacity is lower, not higher. Under-40s miss most (0.38) and are selected most (0.50).
- The smallest region group (`Oyo`, n ≈ 180 in test) is over-selected by ≈ 0.10 (bootstrap 95% interval excludes zero). The generator gives region no causal effect and the region's whole-cohort miss rate matches the others, so this reflects a small, atypical test sample. Because a score only *adds* support, over-selection is the less harmful direction; it is logged as a monitoring item rather than mitigated by a threshold change. Under-selection of a frequently-missing group would block promotion (gate defined in the notebook).

## Limitations

- Synthetic data: behaviours are simulated; magnitudes will differ in any real program. Retrain and re-audit before use.
- Prior-history features need several visits to become informative; the cold-start estimate is a group average and is labelled as such.
- Reasons are from a linear model and describe association, not cause.
- No free-text or SMS content is used as a feature (deliberate, for privacy).

## Maintenance

- Retrain: `make train` (writes artifact, report, MLflow run). Compare `test` metrics with the previous run before promoting.
- Nightly scoring: Celery task `worker.tasks.score_upcoming_appointments` (02:00 Africa/Lagos) appends to `risk_scores`; `latest_risk_scores` exposes the newest row per appointment.
- Drift monitoring and versioned promotion: Step 9.
