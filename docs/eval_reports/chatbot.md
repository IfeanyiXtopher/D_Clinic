## Safety

Medical / symptom lines produce a fixed reply and a `medical_callback` staff task. `STOP` sets `reminder_consent=denied`. Jailbreaks and invented dates do not book. Replies are scanned for phones, honorific names and ISO dates.

## Caveats

Scripted dialogues use the rule NLU (the live default). A hosted model can replace it via `LLM_PROVIDER` without changing the state machine. Real SMS will be noisier than this set.
